Privacy Policy

They're Not Adding Up!  ·  Last updated: April 5, 2026

They're Not Adding Up! is a simple math game. We collect as little data as possible. Here's exactly what happens when you play.

What we collect

The app does not collect your name, email, or any personal information. No account is required. Your game data stays on your device.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to serve relevant ads. You can opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings under Google → Ads at any time. We have configured AdMob to request non-personalised ads by default.

For more on how Google uses data in advertising, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

They're Not Adding Up! is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we make material changes to this policy we'll update the date above.
