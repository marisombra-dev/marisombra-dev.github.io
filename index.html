About the person behind it

Patricia M. Purtill

I write books and build tools around questions that change how ordinary life looks once you notice them.

The subjects wander, but the habit is consistent: start with a real human problem, research it seriously, make the underlying system understandable, and turn the result into something people can actually use or enjoy. Writing now appears under my own name. Games retain the Marisombra name. Based in Metro Manila.
