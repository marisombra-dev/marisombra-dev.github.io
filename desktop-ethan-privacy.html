Desktop Ethan Privacy Policy

Effective: September 16, 2026

Desktop Ethan is a personal desktop AI companion operated locally by its owner. This policy describes how its optional Google Workspace integration accesses and handles Google user data.

Google data accessed

If the owner authorizes the integration, Desktop Ethan may access Gmail messages and metadata, Google Calendar calendars and events, and Google Drive files and metadata to perform tasks the owner requests.

How Google data is used

Google user data is used only to provide owner-requested features such as searching or reading email, reviewing calendar information, locating files, preparing drafts, or performing other explicitly enabled Workspace actions. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale.

Storage

OAuth client credentials and authorization tokens are stored locally in the owner's private user configuration. The public website and public source repositories do not contain access tokens, refresh tokens, OAuth client secrets, or private Google account content.

Desktop Ethan is designed to avoid creating an independent cloud copy of Google Workspace content merely for the connector to function. Temporary technical logs may record operational status or errors, but should not intentionally contain full private message, calendar, or Drive contents.

Sharing

Desktop Ethan does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Google data may be transmitted to service providers that are necessary to fulfill the owner's request, such as the AI service used by the desktop companion, only as part of that requested interaction and subject to the owner's configured services and permissions.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke Desktop Ethan's Google access at any time through their Google Account permissions. Local OAuth tokens can also be deleted from the owner's computer, after which the connector must be authorized again before it can access Google Workspace data.

Security

The connector uses Google's OAuth flow rather than collecting the owner's Google password. The application uses a bounded tool policy so that some higher-risk Google actions are intentionally unavailable even when the underlying OAuth token has broader service permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Desktop Ethan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the public project owner profile at github.com/marisombra-dev.
